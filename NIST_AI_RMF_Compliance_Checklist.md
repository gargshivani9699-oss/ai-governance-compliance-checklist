# NIST AI RMF — Compliance Assessment Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist provides a practical assessment approach based on the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0).

It evaluates how an organization identifies, measures, manages and governs risks associated with AI systems.

**Assessment scenario:** Hypothetical organization using an AI-powered recruitment system.

**Reference:** NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0.

## 2. Assessment Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Implemented | Practice is implemented with supporting evidence |
| Partially Implemented | Practice is partially implemented |
| Not Implemented | Practice is not implemented |
| Not Verified | Evidence is insufficient |
| Not Applicable | Practice does not apply |

## 3. GOVERN — Organizational Governance

**Objective:** Establish accountability, policies, responsibilities and risk governance across the AI lifecycle.

| ID | Assessment Question | Expected Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| GOV-01 | Are AI governance policies established and approved? | Approved AI governance policy | Not Verified |
| GOV-02 | Are roles and responsibilities for AI risk management assigned? | RACI matrix or responsibility register | Not Verified |
| GOV-03 | Is accountability for AI-related decisions defined? | Governance structure | Not Verified |
| GOV-04 | Are AI risks considered within organizational risk management? | Enterprise risk register | Not Verified |
| GOV-05 | Are employees trained in responsible AI practices? | Training records | Not Verified |
| GOV-06 | Are third-party AI risks governed? | Vendor assessment records | Not Verified |

## 4. MAP — Context and Risk Identification

**Objective:** Understand the AI system, its intended use, stakeholders and potential impacts.

| ID | Assessment Question | Expected Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAP-01 | Is the intended purpose of the AI hiring system documented? | System purpose document | Not Verified |
| MAP-02 | Are affected stakeholders identified? | Stakeholder register | Not Verified |
| MAP-03 | Are potential harms to candidates identified? | AI impact assessment | Not Verified |
| MAP-04 | Are system limitations and assumptions documented? | System documentation | Not Verified |
| MAP-05 | Are data sources and data characteristics documented? | Data inventory | Not Verified |
| MAP-06 | Are deployment context and human-AI interactions assessed? | Workflow documentation | Not Verified |

## 5. MEASURE — Risk Analysis and Evaluation

**Objective:** Measure AI system risks through testing, evaluation and monitoring.

| ID | Assessment Question | Expected Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEA-01 | Is the AI system tested for demographic bias? | Bias testing report | Not Verified |
| MEA-02 | Is model accuracy evaluated? | Model performance report | Not Verified |
| MEA-03 | Are privacy and security risks assessed? | Security and privacy assessment | Not Verified |
| MEA-04 | Is explainability evaluated for relevant AI decisions? | Explainability assessment | Not Verified |
| MEA-05 | Are testing results documented and reviewed? | Testing records | Not Verified |
| MEA-06 | Are performance limitations and uncertainty assessed? | Model evaluation documentation | Not Verified |

## 6. MANAGE — Risk Treatment and Monitoring

**Objective:** Prioritize risks, implement responses and monitor AI system performance.

| ID | Assessment Question | Expected Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAN-01 | Are identified AI risks prioritized? | Risk prioritization matrix | Not Verified |
| MAN-02 | Are mitigation measures assigned to responsible owners? | Risk treatment plan | Not Verified |
| MAN-03 | Are corrective actions tracked until closure? | Corrective action register | Not Verified |
| MAN-04 | Is human oversight implemented for high-impact decisions? | Human review records | Not Verified |
| MAN-05 | Is the AI system continuously monitored? | Monitoring reports | Not Verified |
| MAN-06 | Are incidents and unexpected AI outcomes documented? | AI incident register | Not Verified |
| MAN-07 | Are AI systems reassessed after significant changes? | Change and reassessment records | Not Verified |

## 7. Evidence Assessment Principles

1. Policies demonstrate documented governance intent.
2. Operational records help demonstrate whether controls are functioning.
3. Testing evidence supports conclusions about AI system performance.
4. Missing evidence should not automatically be classified as proof of control failure.
5. Risk treatment effectiveness must be assessed before residual risk is reduced.

## 8. Assessment Limitations

This checklist is a self-developed educational assessment aid based on selected NIST AI RMF 1.0 concepts and subcategories.

The questions are paraphrased assessment prompts, not an official NIST certification checklist. NIST AI RMF is a voluntary risk management framework, not a certification standard.

## 9. Conclusion

The NIST AI RMF assessment provides a structured way to examine AI governance maturity, identify potential risks and recommend improvements across the AI lifecycle.
