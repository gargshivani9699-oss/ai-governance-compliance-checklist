# AI Governance Compliance Checklist

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents a practical AI Governance Compliance Assessment Framework based on ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF).

The purpose is to evaluate an organization's AI governance practices, identify compliance gaps, assess control implementation, and recommend corrective actions.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Assess AI governance policies and organizational accountability.
- Evaluate AI risk management practices.
- Review transparency, fairness, privacy and security controls.
- Identify gaps in governance and compliance documentation.
- Develop actionable recommendations and corrective action plans.
- Establish an evidence-based compliance assessment approach.

## 3. Frameworks Used

### ISO/IEC 42001

An international standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).

### NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The framework organizes AI risk management into four functions:

- GOVERN
- MAP
- MEASURE
- MANAGE

## 4. Assessment Scope

The assessment covers:

- AI governance policies and accountability
- AI system risk management
- Data governance and privacy
- Fairness and bias management
- Transparency and explainability
- Human oversight
- AI security and access controls
- Monitoring and continuous improvement

## 5. Assessment Methodology

The assessment follows these stages:

1. Define the assessment scope.
2. Identify applicable governance requirements.
3. Develop compliance checklist questions.
4. Identify expected controls and supporting evidence.
5. Assess control implementation.
6. Identify compliance gaps.
7. Prioritize findings based on risk.
8. Recommend corrective actions.
9. Define monitoring and review mechanisms.

## 6. Compliance Assessment Status

The following assessment categories will be used:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Compliant | Requirement is implemented and supported by evidence |
| Partially Compliant | Requirement is partially implemented |
| Non-Compliant | Requirement is not adequately implemented |
| Not Verified | Sufficient evidence is unavailable |
| Not Applicable | Requirement does not apply to the assessment scope |

## 7. Project Deliverables

1. ISO 42001 Compliance Checklist
2. NIST AI RMF Compliance Checklist
3. AI Governance Control Matrix
4. Compliance Gap Assessment
5. Compliance Action Plan

## 8. Expected Outcomes

- Improved understanding of AI governance requirements.
- Structured identification of compliance gaps.
- Better accountability and control ownership.
- Evidence-based compliance assessment.
- Practical recommendations for AI governance improvement.

## 9. Disclaimer

This is a self-developed educational portfolio project using a hypothetical organizational assessment scenario. It is not an official ISO certification audit, independent certification, legal compliance opinion, or representation of an actual organization's compliance status.
